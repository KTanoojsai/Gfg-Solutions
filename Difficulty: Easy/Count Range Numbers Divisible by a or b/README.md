<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/number-of-divisors-in-a-given-range3738/1?page=8">Count Range Numbers Divisible by a or b</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Easy</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);"><p><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Given four integers l, r, a, and b, representing the inclusive range<strong> [l, r]</strong>, find the number of integers in this range that are divisible by either a or b.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Input:</strong> l = 5, r = 11, a = 4, b = 6
<strong>Output: </strong>2
<strong>Explanation: </strong>6 and 8 lie in the range and are also either divisible by 4 or 6.</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Input: </strong>l = 1, r = 3, a = 2, b = 3
<strong>Output: </strong>2
<strong>Explanation: </strong>2 and 3 lie in the range and are also either divisible by 2 or 3.</span></pre>
