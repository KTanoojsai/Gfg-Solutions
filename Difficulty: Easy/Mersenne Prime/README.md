<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/mersenne-prime1438/1?page=9">Mersenne Prime</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Easy</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);"><p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Mersenne Prime is a prime number that is one less than a power of two.&nbsp;</span><span style="font-size: 18px;">In other words, any prime is Mersenne Prime if it is of the form 2<sup>k</sup>-1 where k is an integer greater than or equal to 2. First few Mersenne Primes are 3, 7, 31 and 127.<br></span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;">The task is to find&nbsp;all Mersenne Primes smaller than equal to&nbsp;an input positive integer n.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples :</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>n = 10
<strong>Output: </strong>3 7
<strong>Explanation: </strong>3 and 7 are only &lt;= n primes in the form of 2<sup>k</sup>&nbsp;- 1. </span>
</pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>n = 100
<strong>Output: </strong>3 7 31
<strong>Explanation: </strong>3, 7 and 31 are only &lt;= n primes which are in the form of 2<sup>k</sup>&nbsp;- 1.</span>
