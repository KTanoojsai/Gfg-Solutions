<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/nearest-power0840/1?page=9">Nearest Power</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Easy</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);"><p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Given two integers <strong>n </strong>and <strong>m</strong>, find an integer which is a power of <strong>m </strong>and is nearest to <strong>n</strong>. If two powers of <strong>m </strong>are equally near to <strong>n</strong>, return the greater one.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>n = 6, m = 3<strong>
Output: </strong>9<strong>
Explanation: </strong>Both 3 (3<sup>1</sup>) and 9 (3<sup>2</sup>) are equally near to 6. Since 9 is greater, the output is 9.</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>n = 3, m = 2<strong>
Output: </strong>4<strong>
Explanation: </strong>Both 2 (2<sup>1</sup>) and 4 (2<sup>2</sup>) are equally near to 3. Since 4 is greater, the output is 4.</span></pre>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Constraints:</strong></span><br><span style="font-size: 18px;">1 ≤&nbsp; n , m ≤&nbsp; 10<sup>4</sup></span></p></div><br><p><span style=font-size:18px><strong>Topic Tags : </strong><br><code>Mathematical</code>&nbsp;
