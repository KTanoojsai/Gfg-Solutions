<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/sphenic-number5922/1?page=8">Sphenic Number</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Easy</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);"><p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Given a positive integer <strong>n</strong>, r</span><span style="font-size: 18px;">eturn </span><strong style="font-size: 18px;">true </strong><span style="font-size: 18px;">if n is a Sphenic Number; otherwise, return </span><strong style="font-size: 18px;">false</strong><span style="font-size: 18px;">.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;">A number is called a <strong>Sphenic Number</strong> if it is the product of exactly three distinct prime numbers. </span></p>
<p>&nbsp;<span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>n = 30
<strong>Output: </strong>true
<strong>Explanation: </strong>30 = 2 * 3 * 5 so n is product of 3 distinct prime numbers.</span>
</pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>n = 60
<strong>Output: </strong>false
<strong>Explanation: </strong>60 = 2 * 2 * 3 * 5 so n is product of 4 prime numbers.</span>
</pre>
